Student Organizations

UST - Computer Science Society

The Computer Science Society (CSS) helps students learn how to code and build software. The group runs programming workshops and contests to help Computer Science majors get ready for jobs.

UST - Information Systems Society

The Information Systems Society (ISS) helps students learn how to use technology to solve business problems. The group teaches Information Systems majors how to manage company data.

UST - Society of Information Technology Enthusiasts

The Society of Information Technology Enthusiasts (SITE) helps students develop essential technical skills. The organization provides competent, ethical, and dedicated service through collaborative effort.

UST CNAG - CICS

CNAG-CICS helps students learn about computer networks and internet security. The group offers study tools to help IT and computer science majors prepare for official Cisco tech tests, like the CCNA.

UST Pax Romana - ICS Unit

The Pax Romana – CICS Unit is the official Catholic student group for tech majors at UST. The group runs prayer events, community service projects, and mass celebrations.

Patron Saint of CICS

St. Vincent Ferrer

Feast Day: April 5

St. Vincent Ferrer was a 14th-century Spanish Dominican friar who gained widespread acclaim across Western Europe as a charismatic missionary, logician, and preacher. Known as the "Angel of the Judgment" due to his fiery sermons on repentance and the end times, he was also celebrated for working numerous miracles and for his efforts to help resolve the Western Schism.


As a renowned 14th-century Dominican friar and logician, his brilliant intellectual background serves as an inspiration for students pursuing modern technology, programming, and computer sciences. Every year on April 5, the CICS community honors his legacy by celebrating his feast day with specialized religious and academic activities.
